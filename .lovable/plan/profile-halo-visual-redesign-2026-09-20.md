# Profile Halo Visual Redesign

## Goal
Redesign the existing editor around the selected “Professional workspace layout”: a clean, guided split-screen tool with the preview always visible and controls organized into three clear steps. Preserve all current photo editing, overlay customization, privacy, platform presets, and export behavior.

## Visual Direction
- Use the locked Signal Blue palette: soft gray workspace, white surfaces, `#1264A3` primary blue, deep ink text, and restrained coral accents.
- Replace the current editorial styling with Sora headings and Manrope interface text.
- Keep Profile Halo’s own identity; do not reproduce LinkedIn’s logo, exact brand color, navigation, feed patterns, iconography, or distinctive page composition.
- Use compact professional controls, subtle borders and shadows, restrained corner radii, and short purposeful transitions.

## Layout and Experience
- Introduce a slim branded header with an always-visible local-processing privacy assurance.
- Make the editor itself the primary first-screen experience, removing the oversized marketing-style introduction.
- Build a unified split workspace: large preview and direct photo controls on the left, guided editing controls on the right.
- Organize the right side as Step 1 Photo, Step 2 Frame, and Step 3 Export, preserving every existing option.
- On smaller screens, stack the preview above the controls with comfortable touch targets and no clipped text or overlapping controls.
- Keep the empty upload state clear; after upload, keep drag-to-reposition, zoom, rotation, flip, reset, replace, and start-over actions easy to find.

## Functional Preservation
- Keep all photo work entirely in the browser and retain object-URL cleanup.
- Preserve all overlay presets, custom message, ring and text colors, and text-size controls.
- Preserve LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, and custom export dimensions; aspect-ratio locking; PNG/JPG options; JPG quality; size estimates; source links; and over-limit warnings.
- Keep exports rendered from the original-resolution image rather than the preview.

## Verification
- Check the empty and uploaded states at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Verify photo movement, zoom, rotation, flip, reset, overlay changes, custom colors/text, platform presets, custom dimensions, PNG/JPG export, and disabled states.
- Confirm privacy messaging remains prominent and the page builds without errors.
